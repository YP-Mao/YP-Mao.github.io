Courses Taught

  • Thermochemical Conversion Technology
    Undergraduate Course
    Covers the fundamental principles and engineering applications of thermochemical conversion of solid waste and biomass, including pyrolysis, gasification, and combustion.
  • Microwave Energy Applications in Environmental Engineering
    Graduate Course
    Focuses on the interaction mechanisms between microwaves and matter, microwave multiphase catalysis, and microwave-induced plasma technologies for pollution control and resource recovery.
  • Advanced Environmental Thermal Engineering
    Graduate Course
    Explores advanced theories and technologies in environmental thermal engineering, emphasizing low-zero carbon technologies and energy-efficient processes.
